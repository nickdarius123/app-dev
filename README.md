
# Fast & Furious 7

### A Movie I Enjoy Watching

One of the movies I really enjoy is **Fast & Furious 7**. It is an action movie about cars, racing, friendship, and family. The movie was released in 2015.

## What Makes It Special?

For me, this movie is exciting because of the car races and dangerous missions. I also enjoy watching the characters work together. It is not only about racing but also about caring for your friends and family.

### Characters I Remember

- **Dominic Toretto** - The leader of the group.
- **Brian O'Conner** - A former police officer and Dom's close friend.
- **Letty Ortiz** - A strong and brave member of the team.
- **Roman Pearce** - The funny member of the group.
